# Hormozi frameworks for ICP work

Distilled from *$100M Offers* and *$100M Leads*. Use these as the reasoning spine — cite the framework by name in the brief so the reader sees the logic.

## 1. The market comes first

> "A starving crowd beats a great offer or great persuasion."

Order of leverage: **Market > Offer > Persuasion.** A brilliant message to the wrong crowd loses. Before refining who-to-target language, confirm the *segment itself* is worth attacking. Score any candidate segment on the four traits — a hard fail on any one is a reason to narrow or move:

| Trait | Question | Red flag |
|---|---|---|
| **Pain** | Do they have a painful, urgent, expensive problem you solve? | "Nice to have." No budget urgency. |
| **Purchasing power** | Can they actually pay, and decide to? | Right person, no budget / no authority. |
| **Easy to target** | Can we find them as a discrete group — lists, communities, titles, signals? | Diffuse, no shared watering hole. |
| **Growing** | Is the segment expanding (tailwinds, funding, hiring)? | Shrinking or stagnant niche. |

## 2. Niche down — "the riches are in the niches"

Going narrower *raises* price and response rate because the message can be specific. Push past "everyone who could use this" to the slice where you have an **unfair advantage helping** — fastest result, lowest effort, best proof. That slice is the ICP seed. Broaden later from a position of proof, never start broad.

## 3. Anchor on who you help *most* (not who's easiest to reach)

The strongest ICP is reverse-engineered from your **best existing customers** — most profitable, happiest, fastest to outcome, lowest support burden. Find the common denominators among those accounts; that pattern *is* the ICP. This is the data-pull step in the workflow.

## 4. The Value Equation → the Dream Outcome

Value = (**Dream Outcome** × **Perceived Likelihood of Achievement**) ÷ (**Time Delay** × **Effort & Sacrifice**).

For ICP purposes you mainly need the **Dream Outcome**: the after-state the buyer actually wants. Frame the ICP around it:

> "We help **[specific who]** go from **[painful before]** to **[dream after]** without **[the effort/sacrifice they dread]**."

The persona's fears, status concerns, and what they're measured on feed the Likelihood and Effort terms — capture them so outreach can speak to them.

## 5. Where the dream customers congregate

From the lead-gen side: you reach people through the places and identities they already belong to. This is the basis for **tribes** — the shared affiliations that make an opener feel like one peer to another, not a vendor to a target. Hormozi's point: meet them where they already are and lead with what you genuinely share.

Tribe categories to mine: alma mater, former employer ("ex-Stripe"), hometown/region, accelerator/cohort, community or Slack/Discord, conference attended, mutual connection, shared investor/portfolio, niche professional identity, hobby/sport visible on their profile.

**Rule:** the commonality must be true and specific. A fabricated or generic "tribe" ("we're both on LinkedIn!") destroys the exact trust this is meant to build.
