# YouTube SEO: the rules and their sources

Read on 2026-10-18 by a research agent; every claim carries its label and URL. Labels: OFFICIAL
(YouTube or Google page), STUDY (measured, sample stated), PRACTITIONER (opinion). The agent read
machine summaries of the pages; five rules were then checked again against the official page itself
and are marked **(re-checked)**: chapters, hashtags, tags, the synthetic-content list and Test & Compare.
Pages change: before relying on an exact number, open its URL again.

## 1. How search and recommendations rank videos

- Search ranks on three things: relevance, engagement, quality. Weight changes by search type. [OFFICIAL] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16090438 (the old howyoutubeworks search URL redirects here, 301)
- Relevance means how well the title, tags, description and video content match the query. [OFFICIAL] same URL
- Engagement example given: how long people watch a video for that query. [OFFICIAL] same URL
- Quality means channel expertise, authoritativeness, trustworthiness on the topic. [OFFICIAL] same URL
- Results can differ per viewer (search and watch history). YouTube takes no payment for organic rank. [OFFICIAL] same URL
- Recommendations (Home, Up Next) use viewing behavior, likes, dislikes, subscriptions, feedback, satisfaction surveys, and channel reputation and quality. [OFFICIAL] https://www.youtube.com/howyoutubeworks/product-features/recommendations/
- That recommendations page does NOT name watch time or CTR as signals. Do not write "YouTube ranks by CTR" as fact. [OFFICIAL, absence]
- Claim that YouTube optimizes "viewer satisfaction over watch time" comes from interviews with Rene Ritchie, reported second-hand. I could not fetch a primary transcript. [PRACTITIONER] https://rosetta.to/person/rene-ritchie/2023
- Traffic source definitions: Browse features = Home, subscriptions, Watch Later, Trending/Explore. YouTube search = search results. Suggested = suggestions next to or after other videos, and links in descriptions. [OFFICIAL] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1714921
- What the split means for a new channel: YouTube gives no official new-channel advice on this. Inference: a channel with no history gets little Browse traffic, so Search and Suggested are the first doors. Mark as inference, not fact. [PRACTITIONER]
- Old correlation study (n = 1.3M videos, page last updated 2017, so old): comments, views, shares, and length (page-1 average 14:50) correlate with rank. It is correlation only. [STUDY] https://backlinko.com/youtube-ranking-factors

## 2. Title

- Max 100 characters. No invalid characters. [OFFICIAL] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/57407
- Pick 1-2 main words and put them in both title and description. [OFFICIAL] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12948449
- Title, thumbnail and description are the "more important" metadata for discovery (said in the tags context). [OFFICIAL] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/146402
- Misleading titles, thumbnails or descriptions meant to trick users are "malicious clickbait" under the spam policy. [OFFICIAL] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801973 (the fetch summary showed only the clickbait line, not a full "misleading metadata" section)
- Rule for the agent: the title must promise only what the video delivers. A high CTR with low average view duration reads as clickbait and YouTube is less likely to recommend it. [OFFICIAL] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7628154
- Truncation: no official number. Estimates: desktop search about 60-70 chars, mobile about 50-60 chars. Sources disagree. [PRACTITIONER] https://howmanywords.app/blog/youtube-title-character-limit , https://advancedcharactercounter.com/youtube-description-character-limit-and-title-length-guide-2026/
- Keyword placement: put the main keyword and the hook in the first about 50-60 chars. No official rule exists; this is practice. [PRACTITIONER] same URLs
- A TubeBuddy claim ("60-70 char titles get 35% higher CTR") could not be traced to TubeBuddy. Do not use. [PRACTITIONER, unverified]
- Title A/B test (Test & Compare) EXISTS **(re-checked)** for titles, thumbnails, or both. Up to 3 variants. [OFFICIAL] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400
  - Computer only, in YouTube Studio. "Advanced features" must be on. [OFFICIAL] same
  - Not for Shorts, Scheduled Lives. Premieres only after they become long-form. Not for made-for-kids, mature, private or age-restricted videos. Live archives are allowed. [OFFICIAL] same
  - Winner = highest watch time share, NOT highest CTR. Results: Winner, Performed Same, Inconclusive. If no clear winner, the first variant uploaded stays. [OFFICIAL] same
  - Takes a few days up to 2 weeks. If you edit the title or thumbnail during a test, the test stops. [OFFICIAL] same
  - Thumbnails under 720p (1280x720) get downscaled to 480p in a test. [OFFICIAL] same
  - Older page for thumbnails only: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13861714 (says same limits; says Premieres not testable "at this time")

## 3. Description

- Max 5,000 characters. [OFFICIAL] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12948449 and https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/57407
- Put key details in the opening lines. They show before "Show more". [OFFICIAL] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12948449
- Exact number of characters above the fold: NOT given by YouTube. Estimates 120-157 chars. Sources disagree. [PRACTITIONER] https://advancedcharactercounter.com/youtube-description-character-limit-and-title-length-guide-2026/
- Rule: first 1-2 lines say what the video teaches and name the main keyword, in plain words. [OFFICIAL for "opening lines"; PRACTITIONER for the 1-2 line count]
- Keywords in the description: YouTube says writing descriptions with keywords "can help viewers find your videos more easily through search." [OFFICIAL] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12948449
- Each video needs a unique description. [OFFICIAL] same
- Backlinko measured no correlation between a keyword-optimized description and rank for that term (2017 data, old). [STUDY] https://backlinko.com/youtube-ranking-factors
- Do not paste tag lists or repeat sentences in the description. This is prohibited. [OFFICIAL] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6390658 (hashtag page)
- Do not use misleading descriptions (spam policy). [OFFICIAL] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801973
- Link rules: the spam page restricts content made only to push users off YouTube. External links policy was not fetched. [OFFICIAL, partial] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801973
- Corrections go on a separate line starting "Correction:" or "Corrections:", after the chapters. [OFFICIAL] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/57407
- Formatting: bold, italics, strikethrough; bullets start with `*`, `+` or `-` then a space. [OFFICIAL] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12948449
- Descriptions can be written in the "default description" field to repeat across videos. [OFFICIAL] same
- For a company video: put the sources and the company link below the chapters, and say plainly who made the video. Fits both honesty and the policy in section 11.
- Benchmark of 67 B2B sales videos with more than 20,000 views, published 2026-04-19 to 2026-10-19 (Spanish and English searches). 45 % open with text, not a link or an offer; their median views were 94,639 against 42,235 for those that open with a link (mostly creators who sell courses). The 6 company channels with the most views (Notion, Y Combinator, Salesforce, Claude, Arrows, Lenny's Podcast) all open with text. 70 % say "in this video…", 48 % list what it teaches, 48 % say who it is for, 43 % have chapters. Median first line 109 characters. [STUDY, ours, a correlation, not an experiment] Method in SKILL.md step 2.
- Rule from that benchmark: line 1 the problem and a number, line 2 the question and the main phrase, then what you learn as a list, who it is for, a question to comment on, the company's one link, then the chapters.

## 4. Chapters

- First timestamp must be 00:00. [OFFICIAL] **(re-checked)** https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9884579
- At least 3 timestamps. Ascending order. Each chapter at least 10 seconds. [OFFICIAL] same
- Format: list of "timestamp + title" lines in the description, then Save. [OFFICIAL] same
- Your own chapters override automatic chapters. [OFFICIAL] same
- Checkable rule for the agent: lines match `^(\d{1,2}:)?\d{1,2}:\d{2} .+`, first is `0:00` or `00:00`, count >= 3, gaps >= 10 s, ascending. For a 9:35 video use `MM:SS`.
- Key moments in Google Search: for YouTube videos you can set timestamps and labels in the description. Google says "We will prioritize key moments set by you, either through structured data or the YouTube description." Google also tries to detect segments by itself. [OFFICIAL] https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/video
- Whether a given video gets key moments in Google is not guaranteed. No official promise.

## 5. Hashtags

- Hashtags can go in the title and description. Up to 3 hashtags from the description show above the title (the ones YouTube judges most engaging). [OFFICIAL] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6390658
- More than 60 hashtags on a video or playlist: YouTube ignores all of them. [OFFICIAL] **(re-checked)** same
- Hashtags must relate to the video. Unrelated ones can lead to removal. [OFFICIAL] same
- Third parties repeat "15 hashtags" as the limit. Official page says 60. Sources disagree; use the official 60. [PRACTITIONER vs OFFICIAL] https://vidiq.com/es/blog/post/youtube-hashtags/
- Rule: use 3 hashtags at the end of the description, all relevant. The picks are an editorial choice, not a measured effect.

## 6. Tags

- Tags help if the video's subject is commonly misspelled. **(re-checked)** "Otherwise, tags play a minimal role in your video's discovery." [OFFICIAL] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/146402
- Excessive tags in the description go against the spam policy. [OFFICIAL] same
- The search ranking page still lists "tags" among the text YouTube matches to the query. [OFFICIAL] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16090438 (so "minimal", not zero)
- Backlinko: keyword-rich tags had a very weak correlation with rank (2017). [STUDY] https://backlinko.com/youtube-ranking-factors
- Character limit for tags: not stated on any official page I fetched. The commonly quoted 500 total chars is unverified. [Not verified]
- Rule: add at most 5-10 tags: the main phrase, plus real misspellings (for example "prospeccion" without the accent). No stuffing.

## 7. Thumbnail

- Size: 3840 x 2160 recommended, minimum width 640 px. Ratio 16:9. Format JPG or PNG. Max file 2 MB on mobile, 50 MB on desktop (the page lists both). [OFFICIAL] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431
- Checkable rule: use 1920x1080 or larger (at least 1280x720 for A/B tests), 16:9, JPG or PNG, under 2 MB to be safe on every path. The 2 MB safe cap is my reading of the page, not a YouTube rule.
- Test & Compare for thumbnails: see section 2. Up to 3 variants. [OFFICIAL] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400
- Thumbnail must not mislead (spam policy). [OFFICIAL] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801973
- AI help for making a thumbnail needs no disclosure ("production assistance ... thumbnail"). [OFFICIAL] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491
- Official thumbnail design advice (faces, contrast, text size): I found no official page that states it. What works (big face, 3 words or less, high contrast): [PRACTITIONER] only. Not verified.
- Test & Compare picks by watch-time share and takes longer when variants look alike: test very different ideas. [OFFICIAL] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13861714
- Of 500 videos that broke out, 28 % had no text in the thumbnail; the median among those with text was 5 words. [STUDY, vidIQ] https://vidiq.com/research/youtube-thumbnail-study/
- Faces give a modest lift overall, and do worse in the Business niche (1of10, more than 300,000 videos). Shocked faces are not proven. [STUDY] https://www.searchenginejournal.com/do-faces-help-youtube-thumbnails-heres-what-the-data-says/563944/
- Colour, saturation, arrows and circles showed no measurable difference (Eden, 2,960 videos). [STUDY] https://eden.so/blog/what-makes-a-good-youtube-thumbnail/
- A question the video answers (curiosity gap, Loewenstein 1994, https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-2909.116.1.75). Applied to thumbnails it is advice, not measured. [PRACTITIONER]
- Rule: 3 very different ideas (a face, an object, a crowd), 1-5 words or none, numbers only from the video, checked at 320x180 (search) and 168x94 ("up next") among real results, nothing in the bottom-right corner (the duration badge). The three studies above were cited on 2026-10-19 and are observational.
- CTR context for judging a thumbnail: see section 10.

## 8. Captions and language

- Supported caption files: SubRip (.srt) basic only, SubViewer (.sbv), MPsub, LRC. Plain UTF-8. No styling. [OFFICIAL, via search summary] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2734698
- Does uploading your own SRT help search? The two official pages I fetched do not say so. The captions page says captions "allow you to share your videos with a larger audience" and says nothing about search. [OFFICIAL, absent] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2734796
- Claim "own captions rank better" is [PRACTITIONER] only. Upload them anyway for accessibility and viewers; do not promise ranking.
- Automatic captions use the video's default language only. [OFFICIAL] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2734796
- Video language: Studio asks you to set it. Set it to the video's language. [OFFICIAL] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6300772
- Translated titles and descriptions: Studio > Subtitles > pick video > Add language > "Title and description" > Add. Purpose: "your fans can find your videos in their own language." [OFFICIAL] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6289575 and https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6300772
- Auto-dubbing: on by default for eligible creators. Not eligible: over 120 min, little or no speech, undetectable language, too-fast speech, copyrighted content. Spanish is a source or target language (English is the widest set). Dubs cannot be edited by the creator. Dubbing does not translate titles or descriptions. [OFFICIAL] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15569972
- Auto-dub is on by default for eligible channels; a person decides whether to keep it.
- Example for Spanish videos: neutral vs voseo search terms. No official data. Search terms people type are usually neutral ("cómo escribir mensajes en LinkedIn", "mensaje en frío"), while voseo ("escribí", "contactá") is rare in queries. Inference only, check with autocomplete and Trends (section 9). [PRACTITIONER / inference]
- Rule for Spanish videos: keep the title keyword in neutral Spanish ("mensajes de LinkedIn", "correo en frío") and keep voseo in the narration and hook words. Mark as a hypothesis to test with Test & Compare.

## 9. Keyword research

- YouTube Studio Analytics "Inspiration" tab (earlier called Research, and a glossary calls it Trends): type a topic, see "Searched on YouTube", "Watched on YouTube", and "Recent audience activity". "Content gap" flags searches where viewers cannot find enough quality results. [OFFICIAL] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/11962757 (fetch of this page named a "Trends tab" with Breakout videos and did not name Inspiration; the search summary named Inspiration. Names differ by rollout.)
- Insights are limited to some countries, languages and devices. The page does not say whether Spanish and Latin America are covered. Check in Studio. [OFFICIAL] same
- Google Ads Keyword Planner and the Research tab are the tools YouTube itself names for search terms. [OFFICIAL] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12948449
- YouTube search autocomplete: no official page fetched. Practice: type the seed phrase in YouTube search and note the suggestions. [PRACTITIONER]
- Google Trends "YouTube Search" filter: the help page I fetched did not mention the filter (page truncated). Third-party guides say the search-type filter includes YouTube. Values are relative 0-100, not counts. [PRACTITIONER] https://www.humanlevel.com/en/digital-marketing-dictionary/google-trends . Official page: https://support.google.com/trends/answer/4359550 (compare up to 8 groups of terms, 50 terms per group)
- Rule: pick one primary phrase and 2-3 secondary phrases, check each in autocomplete with the country set to each of the audience's countries, and write the choice and the date in the publish notes.

## 10. After publishing

- End screens: only the last 5-20 seconds. Video must be at least 25 seconds. Up to 4 elements on a 16:9 video (other ratios may allow fewer). Types: video, playlist, subscribe, channel, external site (YPP only). [OFFICIAL] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6388789
- Cards: up to 5 per video. Types: video, playlist, channel, link (link needs YPP). Cards show only on computers per that page. Not on made-for-kids. [OFFICIAL] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6140493
- Pinned comment, playlists: I found no official page that sets rules or claims an effect. Use them as viewer help (pin the sources list), not as ranking steps. [Not verified]
- Shorts cut from the long video: no official ranking claim found. Uploads up to 3 minutes that are square or taller count as Shorts. [OFFICIAL, definition only] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400
- CTR: "Half of all channels and videos on YouTube have an impressions CTR that can range between 2% and 10%." Newer channels or videos under 100 views vary more. [OFFICIAL] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7628154
- YouTube also says: do not judge CTR right after upload, ignore small swings, compare across videos by traffic source. [OFFICIAL] same
- Average view duration: no official target. "50% is good, under 40% poor" is a blog rule of thumb. [PRACTITIONER] https://seoroast.com/blog/youtube-ctr-avd
- Retention curve: Studio shows key moments for audience retention (intro percentage, top moments). Top moments appear only for videos of at least 60 s and at least 100 views. [OFFICIAL] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314415
- Rule for the agent: first look at analytics after at least 100 views and a few days; the A/B test needs up to 2 weeks.

## 11. Disclosures and policy

### (a) Altered or synthetic content label

Official page: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491
- Disclose content that "seems realistic" and: makes a real person appear to say or do something they did not; alters footage of a real event or place; generates a realistic scene that did not occur; or creates music that is the main focus. [OFFICIAL]
- No need to disclose "non-realistic content that's made with AI" or minor edits. [OFFICIAL]
- Exempt examples (quote): **(re-checked)** "Using an AI-generated or altered animation of a missile in a fully animated video"; "Someone riding a unicorn through a fantastical world"; "Production assistance, like using generative AI tools to create or improve a video outline, script, thumbnail, title, or infographic"; "Caption creation"; "Cloning one's own voice to create voice overs or dubs"; "Idea generation". The list is "not exhaustive." [OFFICIAL]
- Required examples include "Making it appear as if someone gave advice that they did not actually give" and "AI generated music". [OFFICIAL]
- Older blog post (March 2024) lists "synthetically generating a person's voice to narrate a video" as a likeness example. That is about a real person's voice. [OFFICIAL] https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/disclosing-ai-generated-content
- Reading for a video with a stock AI narrator and cartoon AI images (inference, not YouTube's statement): the AI narrator is a stock synthetic voice, not a real person, so no real person seems to say something they did not. The images are clearly cartoon, so they are "not realistic". So the label is probably NOT required. YouTube does not say this about stock voices in so many words. Sources are partly mixed (help page vs 2024 blog). A human should confirm. Adding the label anyway is allowed and low-risk; I found no rule against it.
- The AI narrator as an "AI persona on sensitive topics" (health, legal, finance) is a separate YPP rule, see (c). The first video (sales outreach advice) was none of those; check yours.
- Condition to keep the "no label" reading true: no real person's face or voice is imitated, and no realistic fake scene appears.

### (b) "Includes paid promotion" box

Official page: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/154235 (en-GB version: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/154235?hl=en-GB)
- Quote: "You have to let us know if you include any of those by selecting the paid promotion box" (the "those" are paid product placements, sponsorships, endorsements). Label appears at the start of the video. [OFFICIAL]
- The en-GB version includes the words "(or for a creator's own brands if the relationship between creator/brand is not clear)" in the endorsement definition. [OFFICIAL] This is the closest official text to a company's video about its own product.
- No sentence on the page exempts a company that promotes its own product in its own video. The same page notes one policy "doesn't apply to videos created by or for brands and uploaded to the brand's YouTube channel" (that is the ad-embedding policy, not the checkbox). [OFFICIAL]
- Related viewer page: sponsorship = "Videos that have been financed in whole or in part by a company." [OFFICIAL] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10588440
- Legal duty is separate: creators must follow local law. Page points to FTC (US), ASA (UK), and others. Not legal advice. [OFFICIAL]
- Answer for a company promoting itself in its own video: NOT proven either way. Nobody third-party paid, so a strict reading says the box is for third-party deals. The "own brands" clause says it can apply when the link between creator and brand is "not clear". When the company says plainly that it made the video, the link is clear. Safer rule: say it in the first lines of the description and in the video itself. Human decides the box. Default suggestion: leave it unchecked and keep the on-screen and description disclosure plain.

### (c) Inauthentic / repetitious content (YPP)

Official page: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392
- On July 15, 2025 "repetitious content" was renamed "inauthentic content". [OFFICIAL] same
- Not allowed: "AI-generated content made with generic or unoriginal templates giving the impression of mass production" and stitching "unrelated or inconsistent AI clips to surprise or shock viewers". [OFFICIAL] same
- Allowed: "using AI to edit your video scripts or generate a unique background visual", and "using AI to visualize a unique character and narrative you invented". [OFFICIAL] same
- AI personas giving health, legal or financial advice as an expert are not allowed. [OFFICIAL] same
- Policy applies to the whole channel, not only one video. [OFFICIAL, from page summary]
- YouTube and its liaison called the July 2025 change a "minor update" to long-standing policy. Second-hand. [PRACTITIONER] https://ppc.land/youtube-clarifies-inauthentic-content-policy-changes/
- Risk: low for a first video (original script, sources, one narrative arc). Risk grows if later videos reuse the same template, voice and art with little change. Rule: each new video needs its own script, its own sources and visible educational value; keep a record of what is new.
- Important: the page is about monetization. The channel can still be monetized only if in YPP. A new channel is not in YPP yet, so this is a future risk.

## 12. Google Search

- Google can show video key moments for YouTube videos using description timestamps. Your timestamps take priority over automatic ones. [OFFICIAL] https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/video
- Clip and SeekToAction markup are for videos embedded on your own site (the company page). Not needed for the YouTube page itself. [OFFICIAL] same
- Eligibility needs Search Essentials and video indexing requirements (not listed in the fetched summary). [OFFICIAL] same
- Rule: embed the video on the company's own page with a short text summary; chapters in the description are the part the YouTube side controls.
- Nothing official says a longer description or more keywords help the Google result. [OFFICIAL, absent]

## 13. Myths (official sources contradict or do not support)

- "Tags are a big ranking factor." YouTube: minimal role, mostly for misspellings. [OFFICIAL] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/146402
- "More tags is better." YouTube: excessive tags break spam policy. [OFFICIAL] same
- "Long descriptions rank better." YouTube gives a limit (5,000) and asks for unique, useful opening lines; no length benefit stated. Backlinko 2017 found no correlation for keyword descriptions. [OFFICIAL / STUDY] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12948449 , https://backlinko.com/youtube-ranking-factors
- "More than 15 hashtags cancel all of them." Official limit is 60. [OFFICIAL] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6390658
- "The best title in an A/B test is the one with the highest CTR." YouTube picks by watch time. [OFFICIAL] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400
- "A high CTR always helps." YouTube: high CTR with low view duration reads as clickbait. [OFFICIAL] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7628154
- "YouTube ranks by CTR and watch time" as the only signals. YouTube's own pages list relevance, engagement, quality, satisfaction, reputation. [OFFICIAL] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16090438
- "Uploading your own SRT boosts search." Not stated in the official captions pages I read. [Unsupported]
- "Pinned comments / playlists boost rank." No official source found. [Unsupported]
- "AI voice always needs the synthetic-content label." Official rule is about realistic content depicting real people or events; clearly unrealistic content is exempt. [OFFICIAL] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491
- "The paid promotion box is for any promotion." Official text names paid placements, sponsorships, endorsements; own-brand case is only touched by the "not clear" clause. [OFFICIAL] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/154235?hl=en-GB

## Not verified / sources disagree

- Title truncation: no official number. Search about 60-70 chars desktop, mobile about 50-60; sources differ. [PRACTITIONER]
- Description characters above "Show more": no official number. Estimates 120-157. [PRACTITIONER]
- Hashtag cap: official 60 vs blogs "15". Official wins.
- Tag character limit: not on any official page I fetched. 500 chars is an unverified folk number.
