# Hormozi outreach frameworks ($100M Leads)

The reasoning spine for *how we message* on each rung. Cite the framework by name in the roadmap so the reader sees the logic. (The lead-magnet theory lives in the `lead-magnet` skill; this file is about **reaching out**.)

## The Core Four

The only four ways to let people know about your offer:

| | **People who know you** | **Strangers** |
|---|---|---|
| **1-to-1 (private)** | **Warm outreach** | **Cold outreach** |
| **1-to-many (public)** | **Post content** | **Paid ads** |

This roadmap covers the **private 1-to-1 column** — warm outreach, then cold. (Posting content is the *positioning* workstream — a parallel track, often a founder's most neglected reach; flag it, but it isn't this roadmap.) The warmth ladder is just: **work the warm column dry before you lean on the cold one.**

> Map to the waves: **Wave 1 & 2 = Warm outreach.** **Wave 3 = cold warmed by a shared tribe.** **Wave 4 = cold warmed by a live signal.**

## Warm outreach (Waves 1–2) — the asset competitors don't have

People who already know, like, and trust you convert far above strangers. Start here; it's the fastest money in the book.

- **Build the list from everywhere (omnichannel).** Pull every contact across every platform — phone, email, LinkedIn, Instagram, WhatsApp. Dormant contacts count: **reactivation** of old relationships is warm outreach too.
- **A-C-A — Acknowledge, Compliment, Ask.** The reconnection script: acknowledge the real connection ("we met at…", "you've been doing X"), give a genuine compliment, then make a low-pressure ask. Personal, not templated.
- **Give before you ask** — "give, give, give, ask." Lead with value (the lead magnet); the ask comes after the give, so the message reads as generosity, not extraction. This is "thank god you reached out" in one line.
- **The CTA is an easy yes.** A question that costs nothing to say yes to — *"want me to send it over?"* — not "book a demo" / "buy now". Lower the friction of the first yes; escalate later.

## Cold outreach (Waves 3–4) — warm it up first

Cold works when five things are present:

1. **A good list** — tightly matched to the ICP (fit signals).
2. **A big, fast value offer** — the personalized lead magnet does the heavy lifting; it makes a stranger's message feel like a gift.
3. **Personalization + an honest reason-to-reach** — this is what converts cold → warm. Signal-led outreach has two honest reasons no generic sender has: a **shared tribe** (Wave 3) and a **live timing signal** (Wave 4). The signal/tribe is the *permission* to be in their inbox.
4. **A low-friction CTA** — same easy-yes question as warm.
5. **Follow-up** — most replies come from the 2nd–4th touch, not the first. A wave isn't "sent", it's a sequence.

### Volume & scaling — More, Better, New
- **Rule of 100:** once a message works, leads are partly a volume game — commit to a high daily count of primary actions. Cold waves earn the right to volume by first proving the message.
- **More · Better · New:** to grow a channel, do **More** of it, make it **Better** (test the message/offer — this is what Wave 3's parallel tribe experiments are *for*), or add **New** channels (omnichannel reach). The winning Wave-3 variant becomes the **Better** baseline that scales into Wave-4 volume.

## The thesis under all of it

> "Give away so much free value that it feels absurd — then buying is the obvious next step."

Every rung leads with a give. Warmth changes the *opener* (relationship → real-world context → tribe → signal) and the *autonomy* (human-approved → templated-with-guardrails), but never the order: **value first, ask second.**
