# Personalized lead magnets — the creative engine

The highest-converting lead magnet is **personalized × high-value × zero-effort**: a done-for-you artifact about *this exact prospect*, built from their own publicly-available data, that they did nothing to receive. This file is how you invent one for any offer.

AI agents can gather the public data and generate the artifact **per prospect at scale**, so you can do what used to be a manual favor — for an entire list. You're designing a **magnet engine** (recipe), not one static asset.

## The mental move

> *"What could we find or compute about this specific prospect, from public sources, that (a) delivers a real win already-done, (b) proves we can solve their problem, and (c) reveals they have the problem?"*

The artifact **is** the proof of competence. If you sell X, give them a slice of X *already done for them*. The icebreaker writes itself: "I made you this."

### The canonical example
A company that **sells lead generation** shouldn't give an ebook. It gives **a report with 10 qualified leads for that prospect — each with a ready-to-send icebreaker.** Zero effort, obviously valuable, and it reveals the next problem (they need 1,000 more, done consistently → the paid offer). Translate this exact move to your offer.

### Translating the move (illustrative)
| If you sell… | The personalized, done-for-you magnet is… |
|---|---|
| Lead generation | 10 qualified leads + a ready-to-send icebreaker each |
| SEO | A mini-audit of *their* site: 5 keywords they're losing + the exact pages to fix |
| Recruiting | 5 sourced candidates already matched to *their* open role |
| Conversion/CRO | A teardown of *their* checkout with 3 annotated friction points |
| Ads | A rewrite of *their* current live ad + why it'll beat the original |
| Customer-support AI | A breakdown of *their* real support tickets / public reviews → what % an agent resolves end-to-end |
| Security | A surface scan of *their* public assets → specific exposures found |

Pattern: **do one real unit of the paid work, for them, from public data.**

## Where to mine public data (be creative — combine sources)

- **LinkedIn / company data (see the `icp-definition` skill's SIGNALS.md):** company firmographics, headcount/growth, the people in target roles, their posts and engagement, job openings, recent hires.
- **Their website:** product/pricing/features pages, positioning, customer logos, careers (what they're hiring reveals priorities & pain), blog/changelog (what they ship & struggle with).
- **Use their public product.** Sign up for the free tier, the demo, the app, the trial. Experience it as a user and *report what you found* — onboarding friction, a slow flow, a missing feature, a confusing screen. This is the richest, least-copyable source.
- **App stores / review sites:** App Store, Google Play, G2, Trustpilot, Reddit — real customer complaints in their own words (gold for support/CX/CRO offers).
- **Public data & tooling:** their job posts (hiring = budget + pain), funding/news, social activity, page speed / SEO / tech-stack lookups, public dashboards, GitHub, status pages.
- **Compute something they don't have:** a benchmark of them vs. peers, a forecast from their public numbers, a scored audit. A *calculated* insight feels like magic.

## Cautions

- **Truthful & accurate.** Everything in the artifact must be real and checkable — a wrong "personalized" finding destroys trust faster than no outreach. Verify before it ships.
- **Helpful, not creepy.** Use only public data, and frame it as *"I noticed / I made you"* — useful and flattering, never surveillance.
- **Must scale.** If generating one takes hours of human work, it isn't an engine — simplify the recipe until an agent can run it per prospect.
- **Still obey Hormozi.** Narrow problem, solved completely, that reveals the next problem → the core offer. Personalization amplifies the framework; it doesn't replace it.
